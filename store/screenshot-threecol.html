Draft · edited just now

Workspaces by name

Every workspace now has a name. Home holds the apps you keep open all day, and a new window asks where it belongs, so each project keeps its windows together.

Three columns

On a wide screen the window you're working in sits in the middle at half the width, with the rest stacked on either side. Search+J and Search+K move between them; Search+Shift+M brings one to the middle.

A launcher for everything

Tap Search+Space to open an app, find any tab, or jump to a workspace by name. The keys you'd use are shown beside each one, so you learn them as you go.

No windows to drag, no gaps to fiddle with: open a window and tide puts it in its place.
